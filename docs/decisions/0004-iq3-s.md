# Serve GSQ-RCO IQ3_S instead of IQ3_XXS, with 6 GiB RAM floors and a 2 GiB ZFS ARC

## Context and Problem Statement

On 2026-10-18 the maintainer asked whether a 4-bit quantization of the model fits this machine, and to use its RAM
to the limit while keeping 6 GiB free: the box serves only this model. The service then ran ISTA-DASLab's GSQ-RCO
IQ3_XXS (see [the deployment record](../deployment-record.md)).

Where each candidate would live (`results/iq3s-memory-budget-20261019.txt`, computed from the GGUF tensor headers by
`bench/model_memory_budget.py` on 2026-10-19; the download sizes are in
`results/iq3s-model-files-20261019.txt`; for IQ3_XXS the per-card sums equal the engine log of the 2026-10-18 02:26
start, 9.41 / 9.88 / 11.43 / 9.25 GiB, read by hand into `results/q4-budget-20261022.txt`; the IQ3_XXS starts in
`results/batch_test_deployed_engine.log` and `results/deploy-0.1.45-20261019.txt` logged the same):

## Considered Options

1. Keep IQ3_XXS.
2. GSQ-RCO IQ3_S.
3. Unsloth UD-IQ4_XS.
4. A 4-bit file: bartowski Q4_0, IQ4_XS or Q4_K_M, or Unsloth UD-Q4_K_XL.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "GSQ-RCO IQ3_S", because it is the better model by the published measurements of the same files
(criterion 3), it was stable in every run here and kept the floors in its robustness run (criterion 1), and the
maintainer asked for the best model and accepted the speed it costs (criterion 2 does not hold).

### Consequences

- Good, because IQ3_S agrees with Q8_0 more often than IQ3_XXS (top-1 87.69% against 84.46%, mean KL 0.144 against
  0.202; QUANTS.md at v0.1.45) and scores higher on ISTA-DASLab's card (GPQA Diamond 92.93 against 91.41,
  LiveCodeBench v6 86.86 against 86.29).
- Bad, because it is slower (`results/AB-iq3s-ab3.md`): prompts of 4.4K and 32K tokens are read 13.16% and 9.61%
  slower, 1, 2 and 4 clients get 1.92%, 6.47% and 6.53% fewer tokens per second, and a 16K prompt that joins three
  decoders gets its first token 9.62% later.
- Bad, because it leaves less memory: in the runs, the least free VRAM was 1094 MiB (CUDA2) against 1938 MiB, and the
  least MemAvailable 16.8 GiB against 21.6 GiB. 2% of the experts are computed by the CPU from page-locked RAM (5.46 GiB,
  against 1.12 GiB).
- Bad, because a start takes longer: `systemctl restart` returned after 550-560 s in IQ3_S's six restarts of the runs,
  against 460-470 s in IQ3_XXS's four (`results/ab2-*-switch.txt`, `results/ab3-*-switch.txt`). IQ3_S's first start
  ever read the experts at 60 MB/s and needed more than 900 s (`results/ab-iq3s-1-switch.txt`); `strata-q3.service`
  allows 1800 s.
- IQ3_XXS's files stay on the disk. Switching back: `bench/switch_model.sh iq3_xxs <record>`, then
  `config/profile.conf`, `config/strata-iq3_xxs.deployed.json` and `config/deployed-artifacts.sha256` as they were
  before this decision.

### Confirmation

- `scripts/check-drift.sh` compares the live config with `config/profile.conf`, the installed units with `systemd/`
  and the host's units with `config/host-units.conf`.
- `config/profile.conf` names `--model iq3_s`, and `config/deployed-artifacts.sha256` lists IQ3_S's pack and shards;
  `scripts/check-drift.sh --full` checks the shards.
- `scripts/check-deployment.sh` checks the ARC cap.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

- **4-bit files do not fit.** The numbers below (`results/q4-budget-20261022.txt`) assume a 6 GiB floor, the ARC at
  2 GiB, 32K context, `--kv-resident 20480` and a 1024 MiB reserve; even then, the experts that VRAM cannot hold do
  not fit in the RAM that is left.

  | File | RAM short |
  |---|---:|
  | bartowski Q4_0 | 1.5 GiB |
  | Unsloth UD-Q4_K_XL | 11.2 GiB |

  - bartowski IQ4_XS would leave 1.2 GiB, with 12% of the routing computed on the CPU.
  - bartowski Q4_K_M's Q5_0 down projections run only on the FP16 prompt path.
- **UD-IQ4_XS fits only without longer context.**
  - With the ARC at 2 GiB, 5.2 GiB is left above the floor, and 8.8% of the routing is computed on the CPU (42 of 480
    expert products per token).
  - Its routed experts are IQ3_S gate/up with IQ4_NL down; only its dense side is Q8_0. Its mean KL against Q8_0 is
    lower than IQ3_S's (0.101 against 0.144), but its perplexity is higher (4.120 against 4.097); both pairs are
    from QUANTS.md at v0.1.45.
  - It needs `--compat-bf16`, which on a Strix Halo put perplexity 6-9% above llama.cpp on the same file
    (UNSLOTH_Q4.md).
- **IQ3_S fits almost entirely in VRAM.** It has 6.87 GiB more experts than IQ3_XXS.
  - At the 1536 MiB reserve with cuts 13 / 25 / 38, the four expert caches hold 98.97% of the expert profile's
    routing, so 4.9 of 480 products per token are computed on the CPU; at IQ3_XXS's cuts, 13 / 26 / 38, they would
    hold 98.67% (`results/iq3s-memory-budget-20261019.txt`). The engine runs IQ3_S at 13 / 25 / 38
    (`results/ab3-iq3s-1-arm.txt`).
  - Its formats (IQ2_S, IQ3_XXS, IQ3_S and IQ4_XS gate/up; IQ4_NL and Q2_0 down) have sm_60 kernels in the deployed
    engine.
- **The VRAM reserve stays at 1536 MiB.**
  - With IQ3_XXS the engine reported 100% of the experts resident on the cards, so the reserve never bound
    (`results/iq3xxs-1-arm.txt`).
  - With IQ3_S the cache fills CUDA2 down to the reserve: it holds 6218 of its 6656 profiled pairs, and the other
    cards hold all theirs (`results/ab3-iq3s-1-arm.txt`).
  - No run here used a 1024 MiB reserve. When a run's requests began, CUDA2 had 1212-1250 MiB free at the 1536 MiB
    reserve and 972 MiB at 1280 MiB (`gpu free MiB` in `results/s2-*-arm.txt`), and during IQ3_S's two robustness
    runs CUDA2's use grew by 476 MiB after the first sample (the peak against the first row of
    `results/ab2-iq3s-robust-robust-monitor.csv` and `results/ab3-iq3s-robust-robust-monitor.csv`). At 1024 MiB,
    CUDA2 would begin with about 720 MiB free (972 MiB less the 256 MiB of reserve) and could fall to about
    240 MiB, below the 395-538 MiB free at which upstream #2065 reports a hang.

## More Information

**Criteria and protocol, amended on 2026-10-18 at 23:36, on 2026-10-19 at 01:15 and again on 2026-10-19 after a fourth
review, all before any IQ3_S measurement.** The first version (commit d95cd7a, 2026-10-18 22:36) measured the baseline
on the engine that had run for 20 hours (`results/iq3xxs-1-arm.txt`), in the order ABAB, judged the speed on the
means only, and ran the quality
subset in the service's default mode with one sample per item. The third review found that the tools would have failed a
stable IQ3_S on lines that are not faults: a request that waits 90 s for a slot (the Poisson phase overloads the 4 slots
on purpose), another client's request, an interrupted run. It also found that
the 300 s were counted from the engine's start, inside the 7-9 minute load (`results/service-load-times.txt`), and
that the values step 1 deploys were
compared between the runs but never checked. The fourth review found that a run measured again could never take the
place of the run it repeated, that the summary failed IQ3_S in two cases this record calls impossible to judge, and that
no step of the protocol ran the robustness run of criterion 1; criterion 1 and steps 1 and 6 below now say how each is
handled. Each finding is now a test in `tests/test_ab_model_summary.py` or `tests/ab_model_run_stubs.sh`. IQ3_S is kept
when criteria 1 and 2 hold, the robustness run included; criterion 3 rests on published measurements (see there).

**Protocol for speed and stability** (`bench/ab_model_run.sh` around the standard perf set, `bench/run_profile.sh`;
judged by `bench/ab_model_summary.py`, whose exit status is the verdict):
1. The host changes below and the 6 GiB floors are deployed first, so that every run has them. The summary checks
   each run against this repository: the ARC cap of `systemd/zfs-arc-max.service`, both floors and the model's layer
   cuts in `tools/configure_strata_q3.py`, the PLE file as shard 2 of the loaded model, `config/profile.conf` naming
   that model, and the host units as `config/host-units.conf` sets them. `bench/ab_model_run.sh` refuses to start a
   run when the ARC cap, either floor or the host units are not so.
2. Four runs in the order ABBA, A the baseline: `ab-iq3xxs-1`, `ab-iq3s-1`, `ab-iq3s-2`, `ab-iq3xxs-2`. The service is restarted
   before every run, so each run has an engine of its own. A run starts 300 s or more after the service became ready
   (the model loaded) and when no request is in flight. `bench/switch_model.sh <model> <record>` does each restart,
   also when the model stays, and switches back to IQ3_XXS when IQ3_S does not come up.
3. serve_load's prompts are the same in every run (nonce `ab-0004`), so a request that runs alone decodes the same
   text in every run. With the default nonce (the clock), two runs of 0.1.44.1 with the deployed configuration served
   35.6 and 33.9 tok/s with 1 client, and within 0.5% of each other with 2 and 4 clients
   (`results/v441-1-serve_load.json`, `results/v441-2-serve_load.json`): the 1-client rate depends on how many drafts
   the decoded text accepts (245 to 263 per 512-token answer in these runs, `results/v441-1-engine-lines.txt`,
   `results/v441-2-engine-lines.txt`).
4. The runs differ in the model only: the summary refuses runs whose other engine arguments, environment, engine,
   server.py, serve_load.py, config without the model's fields, shared settings, service code or perf set (sha256 of
   their files; a commit of results between runs changes neither) differ, or whose model name is not the model the
   engine loaded.
5. The run that was measured before these changes (`iq3xxs-1`'s arm record of 2026-10-18 22:31, with an 8 GiB ARC
   and 8 GiB floors) is kept as a raw result and is not part of the A/B.
6. The robustness run, once the summary of the four runs exits 0, or 1 and the maintainer considers keeping IQ3_S:
   the service is switched to IQ3_S, and 300 s or more after it became ready the robustness set runs once under the
   same wrapper, `bench/ab_model_run.sh ab-iq3s-robust B robustness` (around `bench/run_robustness.sh`). The summary
   judges it with `--robustness ab-iq3s-robust`: the floors of criterion 1 from its monitor
   (`results/ab-iq3s-robust-robust-monitor-all.json`), and the run itself as the others are judged in step 1, with
   the same code, engine and settings as the four runs, measured after the last of them and run to its end. If its
   engine restarts or the kernel logs an Xid or an OOM kill while the floors hold, the summary cannot judge it: this
   record names only the floors for that run, and the maintainer decides.
   Amended on 2026-10-22: the runs were made again under the tags `ab2-…` and `ab3-…` (below), so the robustness run's
   monitors are `results/ab2-iq3s-robust-robust-monitor-all.json` and `results/ab3-iq3s-robust-robust-monitor-all.json`;
   no file has the tag `ab-iq3s-robust`.

**Criteria:**
1. **Stable.**
   - In each IQ3_S run: the same engine at the end; no error line in the engine log, no fault line in the service
     journal (systemd's own lines included), no NVIDIA Xid and no OOM kill of the service or of the whole host; all
     98 requests completed, none cancelled; no failed request.
   - A request that waits for a slot is queueing, not a fault; a slot freed as leaked is a fault. Lost or cancelled
     requests count only when the perf set ran to its end and no other client was active.
   - A baseline run that is not clean makes the A/B impossible to judge, not failed. So does a run with other
     traffic (more than 98 requests, a refused request, a request still running at the end), a run that did not
     finish (interrupted, a client error, a record missing), or a kernel problem that names no process of the
     service: that run is measured again.
   - In the summary a run measured again takes the old run's place (`--remeasured OLD=NEW`) only when the summary
     rates the old run "cannot tell" with no fault, and the report lists each such replacement. A clean run is not
     measured again, and a run with a fault is never replaced: its fault counts.
   - A fault in an IQ3_S run fails the criterion, whatever the other IQ3_S run shows, when that run has no reason
     above to be measured again and no baseline run has a fault or such a reason; otherwise the A/B cannot be judged.
   - During the robustness run on IQ3_S (step 6), every card keeps at least 700 MiB of VRAM free, and MemAvailable
     stays at or above the 6 GiB floor.
2. **Not much slower.**
   - The two baseline runs must agree within 5% on every metric below; otherwise the A/B cannot be judged.
   - On the total decode rate with 1, 2 and 4 clients and on the 4.4K and 32K prompt rates, the mean of the IQ3_S runs
     and each IQ3_S run may be at most 10% below the baseline's mean.
   - On the time to first token of a 16K prompt that joins three decoders, they may be at most 15% above it.
   - Faster is never a failure.
3. **Not worse in quality.** Changed on 2026-10-19, before any quality run: the maintainer chose to spend the local
   time on the comparison with the remote service (every benchmark at 4 samples per item), not on a local quality
   A/B of the two quantizations, which would have taken the service longer still. This criterion
   therefore rests on published measurements of the same files:
   - ISTA-DASLab's model card (revision `ed59f92`): GPQA Diamond 92.93 for IQ3_S against 91.41 for IQ3_XXS, and
     LiveCodeBench v6 86.86 against 86.29.
   - Strata's fidelity against Q8_0 (QUANTS.md at v0.1.45): top-1 agreement 87.69% against 84.46%, and mean KL 0.144
     against 0.202.
   - Both put IQ3_S at or above IQ3_XXS, so the criterion holds unless the speed and stability runs show otherwise.
   - The model that is kept is then measured in the strongest mode against the remote service with `driver2.py`
     (the harness's protocol, scoring and statistics are in its PROTOCOL.md, revised on 2026-10-19 after the third
     review). Its numbers are reported next to ISTA's for the same quantization.

If only the speed criterion fails, the maintainer decides between quality and speed with the numbers in hand.

Added on 2026-10-19, before any IQ3_S run: the summary also reports, without judging them, each run's concurrent-read
lines (decision 0005's concurrent read: the engine prints one for each stretch of a prompt it reads on a thread of its
own), how many of them had slots decoding meanwhile, and the reason the engine gave, if any, for keeping such reads
off. The criteria do not measure how long the other users stop while a prompt is read.
